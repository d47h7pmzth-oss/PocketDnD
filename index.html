⚔️ Pocket DM

Your live-session control centre. Stop flipping through heavy books and endless browser tabs.

  • The "Panic Button": Instant AI-generated NPCs, traps, side quests, and homebrew items that strictly adhere to 5e rules boundaries.
  • Seamless Initiative: A lightning-fast tracker with swipe-to-action shortcuts for damage, health adjustments, and turn progression.
  • The Fatality Guardrail:** Mid-combat warning that triggers if a blow will kill a character, letting you narrative-knockout them instead.

📱 Pocket Player

Your character sheet, reimagined. Complete sheet autonomy without sacrificing the tabletop spirit.

  • Total Autonomy: You control your health, stats, and conditions manually. The app handles the heavy math, but never takes away your choices.
  • Smart Equipment Reader: The second an item is scanned into your inventory, the app updates your Armor Class (AC) and bonuses accurately.
  • Roll Your Way: Use optional in-app digital rolling or roll real, physical dice at the physical table—the app completely adapts to you.

🎒 Frictionless Digital Handouts

No server linking required. Pocket Player and Pocket DM communicate securely via offline-friendly display codes.

1. Create

The DM creates or AI-generates custom loot inside Pocket DM.

2. Scan

A dynamic QR code pops up instantly on the DM's device screen.

3. Equip

The player scans it. The item lands safely in their inventory with zero math.
