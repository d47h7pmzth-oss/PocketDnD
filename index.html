POCKET PLAYER

For players

Loot that moves with the party One scan of your DM's QR code and new gear, gold or even a companion lands on your sheet, stats and all. Done with that dagger? Pass it across the table the same way.
Stats that update themselves Put on armour and your AC changes. Get poisoned and the penalty shows up right where you roll. Strap on armour your class can't handle and it tells you exactly what it'll cost you.
Attacks, fully worked out Hit and damage calculated for you, magic weapon bonuses included, with advantage and crits handled by the rules.
Magic that keeps count Charges, once-per-rest powers and blessings from your cleric, all tracked and rolled for you, and refilled when you rest.
A companion at your side A loyal sidekick or beast companion with its own HP, AC and attacks, ready to roll alongside you.
Every class, done properly All twelve classes, multiclassing that actually adds up, and level-ups that recalculate everything for you.

POCKET DM

For dungeon masters

Scan a stat block, summon a monster Point your camera at a stat block and the creature loads in, ready to fight. No typing, no flicking through books mid-battle.
Initiative in seconds Roll for every monster at once and the turn order lines itself up, so the fight starts while the table's still buzzing.
Battle planned, spoils sorted Decide what every enemy drops before the fight begins. When the dust settles, XP is split per player and the loot is ready to hand out. Nothing forgotten, nothing lost.
Your whole campaign, in your pocket Save every item, monster and creation for your campaign, ready to pull out the moment you need it most.
Loot Beaming Forge magic items, gold, even a loyal sidekick, then beam them onto your players' sheets by QR code.
The Panic Button Players went off-script? Traps, weather, rumours, NPCs, side quests and plenty more, generated in a tap.

Better together. When your DM hands over a shiny new sword, one scan drops it straight onto your sheet. No copying stats out by hand.
