Pocket Player

Your character sheet, done properly — not approximated.

  • Homebrew That Actually Works Build a custom magic item with real spell-attack bonuses, saving throw DCs, even resources that regenerate on a short rest — not just a name and a note.
  • Gear That Calculates Itself Equip a suit of plate and watch your AC update on the spot. Every resource your build actually uses — Ki, Sorcery Points, Superiority Dice — tracked correctly, automatically.
  • You Still Roll Your Own Dice Reading Font support built in, fully playable offline, and sync only if you want it — nothing required, nothing sold.
In Development

Pocket DM

The screen behind the screen, built to disappear.

  • Initiative, No Friction Turns, damage, conditions — swipe through combat without ever breaking stride to hunt for a rule.
  • Instant Reference Every stat block, right when you need it — no flipping through a PDF while six players wait.
  • Generated Encounters Early AI-assisted monsters, traps, and side content, scaled to your party's actual level — a direction we're actively building toward.

Loot crosses the table instantly: hand your players a magic item mid-session, and it doesn't wait for after the game. Pocket DM generates a QR code on the spot, players scan it into Pocket Player, and it's in their inventory before you've finished describing it — no server, nothing leaves the table. Scanning already works today; generation is part of what's being built.
