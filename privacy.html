Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

In short: your stuff stays on your device unless you choose to make an account. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data. The few outside services we use are named below.

Pocket Player

On your device by default. Characters, inventory, spells and notes are stored on your phone. Without an account, nothing is sent anywhere.

Optional account. If you sign up (email and password), your character data is also stored with Firebase (Google) so it can sync across your devices. It's used for nothing else.

Pocket DM

On your device. Your campaign (notes, saved items, monsters, encounters) is stored on your phone. There are no accounts yet. When cross-device sync arrives, we'll update this page first.

AI generators. Some generators (loot, NPCs, monsters, companions) send a text request through our server (Supabase) to Groq, an AI provider, which sends the result back. Requests aren't linked to you, but please don't type personal information into them.

Stat block scanning. When you press scan, the photo you take or choose is sent the same way so Groq's AI can read it. It leaves your phone for this, and we don't store it.

Groq and Supabase. Groq processes requests in the US. It doesn't keep them by default, but may log them for up to 30 days to fix errors or investigate abuse. See Groq's data policy and Supabase's privacy policy.

Both apps

Camera and photos. Used only while you're scanning (QR codes in both apps, stat blocks in Pocket DM), and only after you give permission. QR scanning happens entirely on your phone.

Not collected. No advertising IDs, analytics, tracking, contacts, location or microphone.

Payments. Handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card details.

Beta signup

Your email is stored with MailerLite and used only for TestFlight invites and beta updates. Unsubscribe any time from any email.

Your rights

You can ask to see or delete your data at any time. Pocket Player accounts can be deleted in Settings, and uninstalling removes anything stored on your device. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

The apps aren't for children under 13. If you think a child has made an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes and contact

We'll update this page and the apps if anything changes. For any privacy question, email hello@pocketdnd.com.
