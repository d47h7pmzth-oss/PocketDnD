Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Draft: the Pocket DM section still needs confirming before this page goes live.

The short version: your characters live on your own device unless you choose to make an account. We don't run ads, we don't use tracking or analytics tools, and we never sell or share your information.

What this covers

This policy explains what information is collected by the Pocket Player app, the Pocket DM app, and the beta signup form on pocketdnd.com, why it's collected, and how it's handled. The Pocket Player wording below matches the version inside the app (Settings → Legal → Privacy Policy).

Pocket Player

Character data stays on your device by default

Everything you create in Pocket Player (characters, inventory, spells and notes) is stored locally on your device. If you never create an account, none of it is sent anywhere, and we have no access to it at all.

Optional account and cross-device sync

If you choose to create an account, Pocket Player uses Firebase, a backend service provided by Google, to store your character data so it can sync across your own devices. Creating an account requires an email address and a password. Your character data is then also stored on Firebase's servers, tied to your account. We use it only to provide sync: no analytics on your characters, and no sharing with anyone else.

QR codes

Sharing an item or your Player Card by QR code happens directly between the two devices at your table. Nothing is uploaded to us to make that work.

Pocket DM

Your campaign content (notes, encounters, generated items and anything you build) is stored on your device. Scanning a player's Player Card or sending loot by QR code happens directly between devices at your table.

What we don't collect

No advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics or tracking tools, and no access to your contacts, photos, location or microphone. The camera is used only to scan QR codes, and only while you're scanning. Neither app shows ads.

Payments

Any paid unlock in either app is processed entirely by Apple's or Google's own payment system. We never see or store your card details.

The beta signup form

If you join the beta on pocketdnd.com, your email address is stored with MailerLite, our email provider, and used only to send TestFlight invites and beta updates. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you from future beta emails.

Deleting your data

If you have a Pocket Player account, you can delete it and all its cloud data from inside the app at any time (Settings). If you only ever used local storage, uninstalling the app removes everything. For anything else, just get in touch using the details below.

Children

Pocket Player and Pocket DM aren't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update it here and inside the apps. Continuing to use the apps after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, data access requests or deletion requests, email hello@pocketdnd.com.
